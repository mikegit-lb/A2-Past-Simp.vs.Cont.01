GRAMMAR TOOLKIT · ENGLISH + SMART TURKISH SUPPORT

Collect three grammar clues

CLUE 1

Past Simple vs. Past Continuous

Past Continuous paints the longer background action. Past Simple shows the shorter finished event.

was studying ───── ☎ rang!
While + was/were + verb-ing, + past verb.
When + past verb, + was/were + verb-ing.

While Ece was packing, Mert found a clue.

Türkçe köprü: “-yorken” ≈ while + was/were -ing; “-dığında” ≈ when + past simple.
Watch out: ❌ While Ece packed… → ✅ While Ece was packing…
CLUE 2

Too / Enough

Too means more than we want and causes a problem. Enough means the amount is sufficient.

NOT ENOUGHjust rightTOO MUCH
too + adjective → too dark
adjective + enough → bright enough

The bag is too heavy. The torch is bright enough.

Türkçe köprü: “yeterince parlak” ama İngilizcede sıralama ters: bright enough.
Watch out: ❌ enough warm → ✅ warm enough
CLUE 3

So…that / Such…that

Both structures connect a strong cause and its result. Look for an adjective or a noun.

so + adjective + that + result
such + a/an + adjective + noun + that + result

It was so windy that the ropes shook.
It was such a windy night that the ropes shook.

Türkçe köprü: so…that = “o kadar…ki”; such…that = “öyle…bir…ki”.
Watch out: such needs a noun: such a strange sound.
