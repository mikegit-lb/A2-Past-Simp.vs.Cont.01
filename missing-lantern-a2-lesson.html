GRAMMAR TOOLKIT · ENGLISH + SMART TURKISH SUPPORT

Collect three grammar clues

CLUE 1 · TWO ACTIONS IN THE PAST

Past Simple vs. Past Continuous

What does each tense show?

Past Continuous is the long action already happening. It creates the background—like a video playing.

Past Simple is the shorter completed event. It often enters or interrupts the video—like a camera flash.

was packing ───────── 🔎 found!

While Ece was packing, Mert found a clue.

Meaning: Ece started packing first. Her packing continued when Mert found the clue.

How do I build it?

Past Continuous
was/were + verb-ing
She was looking.
They were walking.
Past Simple
regular verb + -ed / irregular form
look → looked
find → found
While + long action, short event.
When + short event, long action.

Also possible: While Ece was looking, Mert was listening. Here, both actions continued at the same time.

How do I choose?

Ask three questions:
  1. Which action was already in progress?
  2. Which event was shorter or finished?
  3. Do I need was or were?
Türkçe köprü: “-yorken” often matches while + was/were -ing. “-dığında” often matches when + Past Simple.
Common error: ❌ While Ece packed…
✅ While Ece was packing…
Quick check: “I ___ (sleep) when the alarm ___ (ring).”
→ was sleeping / rang
CLUE 2 · TOO MUCH OR JUST RIGHT?

Too / Enough

What do they mean?

Too means more than wanted or possible. It normally introduces a problem.

Enough means the amount is sufficient for a purpose. It normally introduces a successful possibility.

NOT ENOUGHjust rightTOO MUCH

The path was too dark to see.
The torch was bright enough to guide them.

Important: very dark only describes. too dark means the darkness causes a problem.

Where do the words go?

too + adjective
too cold · too heavy · too noisy
adjective + enough
warm enough · strong enough
enough + noun
enough water · enough time

Useful ending: too/enough + to + verb
too heavy to carry
old enough to join

How do I choose?

Follow the result:
  1. Is there a problem? Choose too.
  2. Can the person do it? Choose enough.
  3. Put enough after an adjective but before a noun.
Türkçe köprü: Türkçede “yeterince parlak”; İngilizcede sıra ters: bright enough.
Common error: ❌ enough warm
✅ warm enough
Quick check: “The soup is ___ hot to eat.” → too
“It is cool ___ now.” → enough
CLUE 3 · STRONG CAUSE + RESULT

So…that / Such…that

What do they do?

Both structures connect a strong description with its result. The first half gives the cause; the clause after that gives the result.

It was so windy that the ropes shook.
It was such a windy night that the ropes shook.

Same result, different grammar: Use so before an adjective alone. Use such when the description contains a noun.

How do I build it?

so + adjective + that + result
so tired that I slept
such + a/an + adjective + singular noun + that
such a loud bell that we jumped
such + adjective + plural/uncountable noun + that
such muddy shoes that…
such cold weather that…

Do not add a/an before plural or uncountable nouns.

How do I choose?

Look after the gap:
  1. Only an adjective? Use so.
  2. Adjective + noun? Use such.
  3. Singular countable noun? Add a/an.
Türkçe köprü: so…that = “o kadar…ki”; such…that = “öyle…bir…ki”.
Common error: ❌ such strange that…
✅ so strange that… / such a strange sound that…
Quick check: “It was ___ an exciting trip that I forgot the time.” → such
